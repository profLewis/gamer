## Spell Slots

Wizards, Clerics and Bards gain slots on the same track; Rangers start a level later.

| Level | Wizard / Cleric / Bard | Ranger |
|-------|------------------------|--------|
| 1 | 2 × L1 | — |
| 2 | 3 × L1 | 2 × L1 |
| 3 | 4 × L1, 2 × L2 | 3 × L1 |
| 4 | 4 × L1, 3 × L2 | 3 × L1 |
| 5+ | 4 × L1, 3 × L2 | 4 × L1, 2 × L2 |
