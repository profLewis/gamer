## Class Feature
**Mechanical Insight** — +2 to the Perception check when searching a room, so traps and hidden compartments rarely stay hidden. Gadgets, traps and locks are the Engineer's trade. 3 skill proficiencies.

## Recommended Races
Rock Gnome, High Elf, Human (INT bonuses).
