## Class Feature
**Street Smart** — +2 to haggling, lowering the effective DC on every deal. Quick fingers find the treasure others walk past. 4 skill proficiencies (joint most of any class, with the Rogue).

## Recommended Races
Lightfoot Halfling, Half-Elf, Wood Elf (DEX/CHA bonuses).
