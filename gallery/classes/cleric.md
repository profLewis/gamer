## Class Feature
**Divine Spellcasting** — WIS-based spellcasting, prepare spells from full cleric list.

## Spells
**Cantrips:** Sacred Flame (1d8 radiant, DEX save), Toll the Dead (1d8/1d12 necrotic, WIS save), Spare the Dying (stabilise ally).

**Level 1:** Cure Wounds (1d8+WIS healing, touch), Guiding Bolt (4d6 radiant), Healing Word (1d4+WIS healing, bonus action).

## Recommended Races
Hill Dwarf, Human, Wood Elf (WIS bonuses).
