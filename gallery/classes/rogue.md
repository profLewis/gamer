## Class Feature
**Sneak Attack** — Extra damage dice when attacking with advantage or an ally is adjacent. 4 skill proficiencies (most of any class).

## Recommended Races
Lightfoot Halfling, High Elf, Half-Elf (DEX/CHA bonuses).
