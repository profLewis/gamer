# Half-Elf

| Trait | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Ability Bonuses | +2 CHA, +1 to two other abilities |
| Speed | 30 ft |
| Size | Medium |

## Racial Traits
- Darkvision
- Fey Ancestry (advantage vs charm)
- Two extra skill proficiencies

## Recommended Classes
- Any class — versatile bonuses and extra skills make half-elves excellent in every role.
