Side A · The World Jones Made — Philip K. Dick

Side B · Agent of the Unknown — Margaret St. Clair

Ace Books, 1956 · catalogue number D-150 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-150 (Ace Books, 1956) pairs Philip K. Dick's The World Jones Made with Margaret St. Clair's Agent of the Unknown. In Dick's novel Floyd Jones is a precog who sees exactly one year ahead — a talent that began a year before he was born — and is therefore condemned to live everything twice, once in foresight and once in fact. The bitterness that produces makes him a formidable demagogue against Relativism, the enforced post-war doctrine that no belief may be asserted as true, and he is opposed by Cussick, a Fedgov security agent who stands for ordinary, checkable reality. St. Clair's Agent of the Unknown is set on the pleasure planetoid Fyon, where the drifting Don Haig acquires a small carved doll that weeps continuously; only one other like it exists, and the secret of its maker draws forces that Haig is not equipped for. The DnDex cards Floyd Jones and Cussick both come from the Dick side.
