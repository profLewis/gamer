Ace Double D-449 (1960)

BOOK source card

Source Card

A-side: The Genetic General (Gordon R. Dickson). B-side: Time to Teleport (Gordon R. Dickson). This Ace code uses the dos-a-dos format, so each narrative gets its own front-cover orientation in one physical paperback [1][2].

D-449 is the Ace-double form of Dickson's Dorsai! material: The Genetic General is the revised Ace title linked directly to Donal Graeme's rise among contract-soldier cultures, with Anea Marlivana and William of Ceta as major political actors [3].

Covers and Context

Release records on the Dorsai! page explicitly identify the D-449 pairing as The Genetic General / Time to Teleport. That gives this code a clear place in early Childe Cycle publication history and military-SF development [3][4].
