Ace Books, 1960 · catalogue number D-449 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-449 (Ace Books, 1960) binds two Gordon R. Dickson novels back to back: The Genetic General and Time to Teleport. The Genetic General is Ace's cut text of Dorsai!, serialised in Astounding in 1959 and the first novel of the Childe Cycle. Humanity has split into specialised splinter cultures, and the Dorsai are its professional soldiers, hired out under contract. Donal Graeme leaves Dorsai as a very young officer, is noticed by the financier-politician William of Ceta, and rises through a sequence of campaigns that mark him as a strategic prodigy — and then as something no longer quite human. Time to Teleport is set on an Earth organised into economic Groups, with the Transportation Group's Anthony Sellers manoeuvring to absorb the rest, and a secret society popularly loathed as "the Inhumans" in the middle of it. The DnDex card Donal Graeme comes from The Genetic General. Cletus Grahame is Donal's ancestor in the same Childe Cycle, but he is the protagonist of Tactics of Mistake (1971) and does not appear in this volume.
