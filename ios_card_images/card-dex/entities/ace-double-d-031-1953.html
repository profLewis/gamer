Ace Double D-031 (1953)

BOOK source card

Source Card

A-side: The World of Null-A (A. E. van Vogt). B-side: The Universe Maker (A. E. van Vogt). This Ace code uses the dos-a-dos format, so each narrative gets its own front-cover orientation in one physical paperback [1][2].

D-031 pairs two van Vogt novels that both lean into identity instability and power structures. In The World of Null-A, Gilbert Gosseyn is drawn into the Games Machine and discovers his memories and body are part of a larger manipulation [3].

Covers and Context

The reverse title, The Universe Maker, follows Morton Cargill through time-dislocated politics and the "Shadows" conflict, turning a personal crime into a civilization-scale causal problem [4]. Together they make D-031 one of the most concept-heavy early Ace SF doubles [1][2].
