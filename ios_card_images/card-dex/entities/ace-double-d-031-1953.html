Ace Books, 1953 · catalogue number D-031 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-031 (Ace Books, 1953) is a tête-bêche paperback binding two A. E. van Vogt novels back to back: The World of Null-A and The Universe Maker. The World of Null-A (serialised in Astounding in 1945) opens with Gilbert Gosseyn arriving in the City of the Machine for the annual Games, which test candidates in non-Aristotelian — "Null-A" — logic. The Machine tells him his memories are false: his dead wife Patricia Hardie is alive and is the President's daughter, and every time Gosseyn is killed he wakes up in a fresh, waiting body. The Universe Maker takes Morton Cargill, a soldier who leaves a woman to die in a drunken car crash, and pulls him thousands of years forward so a descendant of his victim can watch him executed; instead he is caught between the future's three factions — Floaters, Tweeners and Shadows — and the question of who is entitled to remake the universe. Both DnDex cards drawn from this volume, Gosseyn and Patricia Hardie, come from the Null-A side.
