Side A · Solar Lottery — Philip K. Dick

Side B · The Big Jump — Leigh Brackett

Ace Books, 1955 · catalogue number D-103 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-103 (Ace Books, 1955) pairs Philip K. Dick's Solar Lottery with Leigh Brackett's The Big Jump. Solar Lottery was Dick's first published novel. In its solar system the highest office, Quizmaster, is assigned by a randomising "bottle" rather than by election or merit, and the incumbent may be legally assassinated. When the bottle twitches Reese Verrick out of office in favour of the elderly Leon Cartwright, the idealistic young Ted Benteley — who has just sworn an unbreakable oath of fealty to Verrick without realising he is no longer Quizmaster — is bound into the plot to kill Cartwright, carried out through an android assassin inhabited in turn by many minds so no telepath can hold a lock on it. Brackett's The Big Jump is an outward-facing counterweight: Arch Comyn forces his way into a guarded hospital on Mars to hear the last words of the only man to come back from humanity's first interstellar flight, and then makes the jump himself. The DnDex cards Ted Benteley, Leon Cartwright and Reese Verrick all come from Solar Lottery.
