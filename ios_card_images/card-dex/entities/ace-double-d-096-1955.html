Ace Books, 1955 · catalogue number D-096 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-096 (Ace Books, 1955) pairs Andre Norton's The Last Planet with Alan E. Nourse's A Man Obsessed. The Last Planet is Ace's retitling of Norton's Star Rangers (1953): the Stellar Patrol ship Starfire, obsolete and posted to the far edge of a galactic civilisation whose Central Control is visibly collapsing, is wrecked on an uncharted world, and its crew has to stop being a military unit and become a settlement. The survivors include Sergeant Kartr, a ranger with strong untrained psionic ability from a planet burned off in the last war, the Zacathan Zinga, and Rolth, whose people are adapted to a lightless world. Nourse's A Man Obsessed (later expanded as The Mercy Men) is a revenge story in a future where medicine is a government monopoly: after a five-year hunt, Jeff Meyer corners Paul Conroe inside the sealed research wards where the desperate sell themselves as experimental subjects. The DnDex cards Kartr Rhyn and Dosvard Rhyn are named from the Norton side's Ranger crew; Kartr is that novel's viewpoint character.
