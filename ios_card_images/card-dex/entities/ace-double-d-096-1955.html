Ace Double D-096 (1955)

BOOK source card

Source Card

A-side: The Last Planet / Star Rangers (Andre Norton). B-side: A Man Obsessed (Alan E. Nourse). This Ace code uses the dos-a-dos format, so each narrative gets its own front-cover orientation in one physical paperback [1][2].

D-096 links Andre Norton's The Last Planet (also known as Star Rangers) with Alan E. Nourse's A Man Obsessed [1][4]. Star Rangers is set during the fragmentation of a galactic empire and uses that decline as the strategic backdrop for its conflict [3].

Covers and Context

A Man Obsessed shifts tone to psychological pursuit: Jeffrey Meyer hunts Paul Conroe through a social collapse shaped by addiction and underground medical experimentation, giving the pair a strong contrast between space-opera structure and dystopian fixation [5].
