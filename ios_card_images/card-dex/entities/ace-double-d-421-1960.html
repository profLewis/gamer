Ace Books, 1960 · catalogue number D-421 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-421 (Ace Books, 1960) pairs Philip K. Dick's Dr. Futurity with John Brunner's Slavers of Space. In Dick's novel Dr Jim Parsons, a twentieth-century physician, is thrown forward into a society organised around youth and a cult of death, where the old are put down and the reflex to save a life is a criminal act. That makes him both an outcast and exactly what one revolutionary group needs: someone who can revive their cryogenically frozen leader — a job that eventually takes the group back to sixteenth-century California and unsettles what they thought they knew about their own history. Brunner's Slavers of Space is set on a comfortable, hedonistic Earth served by androids; the murder of one of them sends Derry Horn out on an interstellar investigation that ends in the discovery that there is no such thing as an android. Brunner later rewrote the book as Into the Slave Nebula (1968). The DnDex card Dr Parsons comes from Dr. Futurity.
