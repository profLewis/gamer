Ace Books, 1961 · catalogue number D-491 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-491 (Ace Books, 1961) binds Fritz Leiber's Hugo-winning novel The Big Time back to back with his collection The Mind Spider and Other Stories. The Big Time is a Change War story told almost entirely in one room: a recuperation station lifted out of ordinary spacetime, where soldiers snatched from every era are patched up between operations in a time war between the Spiders and the Snakes. It is narrated by Greta Forzane, an Entertainer — part nurse, part therapist, part hostess — and turns into a locked-room problem when an atomic device goes missing inside the sealed Place. Among the soldiers who arrive is Bruce Marchant, an RAF pilot who starts arguing that the war itself should be abandoned. The Mind Spider and Other Stories gathers further Change War short fiction on the reverse side. The DnDex cards Greta Forzane and Bruce Marchant both come from The Big Time.
