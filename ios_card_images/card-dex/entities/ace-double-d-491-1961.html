Ace Double D-491 (1961)

BOOK source card

Source Card

Ace Double D-491 (1961) pairs two Fritz Leiber works in one dos-a-dos paperback: The Big Time on one side and The Mind-Spider and Other Stories on the reverse. The format was marketed as a two-front-cover SF value edition [1][2].

The Big Time centers on the Change War between the Spiders and the Snakes, with Greta Forzane narrating life inside a recovery station outside normal time. The cast mixes recruits from different eras, which gives the book its layered historical texture [3][5].

Covers and References

The UNCW archive also documents preliminary Emshwiller art tied to D-491, while bookseller and bibliographic records confirm the Ace code, 35-cent pricing, and 1961 first-print paperback context [2][4].
