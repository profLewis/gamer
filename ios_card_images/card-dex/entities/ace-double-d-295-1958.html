Ace Double D-295 (1958)

BOOK source card

Source Card

A-side: Big Planet (Jack Vance). B-side: The Slaves of the Klau (Jack Vance). This Ace code uses the dos-a-dos format, so each narrative gets its own front-cover orientation in one physical paperback [1][2].

D-295 is a Jack Vance paired code. Big Planet tracks Claude Glystra after orbital sabotage strands his mission far from Earth Enclave; the antagonist Charley Lysidder and companion Nancy are central to the long overland campaign [3].

Covers and Context

Ace documentation lists The Slaves of the Klau as the reverse title for the same code-year. In publication history, D-295 is one of the key points where Vance's large-world social design and serialized magazine material met mass-market double distribution [1][2][3].
