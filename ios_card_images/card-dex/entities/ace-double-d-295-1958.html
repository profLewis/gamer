Ace Books, 1958 · catalogue number D-295 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-295 (Ace Books, 1958) binds two Jack Vance novels back to back: Big Planet and Slaves of the Klau. Big Planet is set on a world enormous in diameter but poor in heavy metals, so technology never takes hold and the surface has filled up with thousands of small, mutually strange cultures. Claude Glystra leads an Earth commission sent to deal with the slave trade and gun-running of Charley Lysidder, the Bajarnum of Beaujolais; the commission is brought down tens of thousands of miles from the single Earth enclave, and the book is the walk back, one incompatible society at a time. Slaves of the Klau follows Roy Barch, taken off Earth into forced labour by the militarist Klau and escaping to a band of runaways in the mountains who are tolerated only because they make convenient quarry for Klau hunting parties. The DnDex card Claude Glystra is Big Planet's protagonist. Woudiver is also a Vance name, but Aila Woudiver belongs to the later Planet of Adventure books, not to this double.
