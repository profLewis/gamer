Side A · The Man with Nine Lives — Harlan Ellison

Side B · A Touch of Infinity — Harlan Ellison

Ace Books, 1959 · catalogue number D-413 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-413 (Ace Books, 1959) binds two early Harlan Ellison books back to back: the novel The Man with Nine Lives and the collection A Touch of Infinity. The Man with Nine Lives is a revenge story in which the protagonist, Emory, is driven across a string of worlds; Ellison disliked the Ace title and reissued a revised text in 2011 under his own preferred title, The Sound of a Scythe. A Touch of Infinity collects Ellison's early magazine short fiction from the same period, making this one of the doubles that pairs an author with himself rather than setting two writers against each other. The DnDex card Nine Lives is named from the title novel. The card Krantz also sits in this group, but no character of that name could be verified in either half of this volume, so no origin is claimed for it here.
