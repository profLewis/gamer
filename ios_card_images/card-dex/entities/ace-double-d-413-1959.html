Ace Double D-413 (1959)

BOOK source card

Source Card

A-side: The Man With Nine Lives (Harlan Ellison). B-side: A Touch of Infinity (Harlan Ellison). This Ace code uses the dos-a-dos format, so each narrative gets its own front-cover orientation in one physical paperback [1][2].

D-413 is an all-Ellison pairing and is bibliographically distinctive because one side is a compact novel and the other a short-story collection. The code-year and two-cover setup are confirmed in both collector and archive listings [1][2][4].

Covers and Context

The Man With Nine Lives is described in Ellison directories as a revenge narrative involving Emory and serial identity-shifts across alien bodies, while A Touch of Infinity includes early SF stories such as Life Hutch, where a stranded pilot faces a lethal malfunctioning robot [3][5].
