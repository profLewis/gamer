Ace Books, 1954 · catalogue number D-053 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-053 (Ace Books, 1954) binds Murray Leinster's Gateway to Elsewhere back to back with A. E. van Vogt's The Weapon Shops of Isher. In Leinster's novel an ordinary American, Tony Gregg, comes into possession of a coin struck in Barkut — a country on no map of Earth — and finds it is the key to a fourth-dimensional world where the djinn of the Arabian Nights are a practical problem. Van Vogt's The Weapon Shops of Isher (1951) is set in the year 4784, in an interstellar empire ruled by the Empress Innelda Isher. Against her stand the Weapon Shops: storefronts that appear where they are needed, selling defensive energy guns that cannot be fired in aggression, founded and quietly steered for millennia by Robert Hedrock, the one immortal man, who has repeatedly married into the House of Isher he is checking. The DnDex cards Robert Hedrock and Innelda Isher both come from the van Vogt side.
