Side A · The Cosmic Puppets — Philip K. Dick

Ace Books, 1957 · catalogue number D-249 · tête-bêche double paperback, the two novels bound back to back and inverted so each has its own front cover. No cover scan is held locally: Ace Double cover art remains in copyright.

Ace Double D-249 (Ace Books, 1957) pairs Philip K. Dick's The Cosmic Puppets with Sargasso of Space, published under Andre Norton's "Andrew North" byline. In Dick's novel Ted Barton breaks off a holiday to revisit his home town of Millgate, Virginia, and finds a town that matches nothing he remembers — including town records stating that Ted Barton died of scarlet fever at the age of nine. He cannot leave, and the real Millgate turns out to be present underneath the false one, held down by two ancient powers using the valley as a board.
